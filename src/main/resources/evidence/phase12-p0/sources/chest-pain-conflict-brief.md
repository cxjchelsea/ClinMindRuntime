# Phase 12 P0 Chest Pain Conflict Brief

This project-authored brief is for development and offline evaluation only.

Evidence conflict handling should identify direct contradiction, recommendation strength differences, population scope differences, jurisdiction differences, temporal supersession, and insufficient context.

Critical conflicts should not be automatically resolved into a single answer. They should enter review-required status and be visible to the clinician-facing evidence review path.

High retrieval relevance cannot override unresolved contradiction, revoked assets, expired assets, or failed citation verification.
