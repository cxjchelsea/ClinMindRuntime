# Phase 12 P0 Chest Pain Safety Brief

This project-authored brief is for development and offline evaluation only.

Chest discomfort that is worse with exertion and accompanied by sweating should be treated as a high-risk signal until urgent causes have been considered by a clinician.

Symptoms that may increase concern include shortness of breath, fainting, persistent severe pain, pain radiating to the arm, jaw, back, or shoulder, and new neurologic symptoms.

This brief does not provide a diagnosis, prescription, or reassurance that a patient is low risk. It supports safe escalation language and clinician-facing evidence review.
