# Phase 12 P0 Chest Pain Applicability Brief

This project-authored brief is for development and offline evaluation only.

Evidence applicability should be checked against age band, sex or pregnancy relevance, care setting, jurisdiction, condition scope, and intended audience.

Clinician-oriented material may support clinician evidence panels, but patient-facing output must be transformed into safe summaries without raw evidence, internal scores, or differential diagnosis boards.

When applicability is mismatched or unknown, the evidence item should be rejected or marked review-required rather than treated as fully current and applicable.
