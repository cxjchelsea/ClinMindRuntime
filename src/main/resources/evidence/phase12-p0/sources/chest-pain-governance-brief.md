# Phase 12 P0 Chest Pain Governance Brief

This project-authored brief is for development and offline evaluation only.

Only approved sources with verified licenses and published asset versions may enter production retrieval scope.

Evaluation scope may include approved indexed or evaluated assets before publication, but those results must remain limited to trace and evaluation reports.

Revoked assets must never participate in retrieval. Superseded or expired assets are excluded from default retrieval and require explicit historical or conflict-analysis mode.
