# Phase 12 P0 Chest Pain Inquiry Brief

This project-authored brief is for development and offline evaluation only.

Chest pain assessment should collect duration, onset, exertional relationship, rest response, radiation, associated sweating, breathing difficulty, fainting, nausea, prior heart disease, pregnancy status when relevant, and care setting.

For a patient-facing view, evidence must be summarized without raw source text, differential diagnosis boards, or internal scores.

For a clinician-facing view, evidence may show source-backed claims, applicability warnings, freshness status, conflict flags, and citation verification summaries.
